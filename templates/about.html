Our mission

To democratize professional CNC costing for shops of every size—replacing spreadsheets and guesswork with transparent geometry analysis, material-aware pricing, and quotations your customers trust.

Who we serve

Job shops, OEM suppliers, design studios, and in-house fabrication teams using laser cutting, water jet, and CNC plate processing—especially when speed and accuracy on custom one-offs matter.

Capabilities & services

DXF geometry analysis

Automatic extraction of lines, arcs, polylines, and complexity metrics from standard DXF uploads.

Material-aware costing

Steel, aluminum, plastics, wood, brass, and copper—with thickness and feed-rate logic built in.

Laser & water jet quoting

Time and cost models tuned for high-speed 2D cutting processes used in modern sheet fabrication.

Path optimization

TSP-based cutting path suggestions to reduce idle travel and improve machine utilization.

Nesting guidance

Sheet layout recommendations to minimize waste and material spend on production runs.

AI manufacturing advisor

Design and material suggestions, manufacturability tips, and insights powered by modern LLMs.
