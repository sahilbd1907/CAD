How per-part cutting cost is calculated

Inside the dashboard, each DXF quote uses the same logic as Indian job-shop estimating:

1. Geometry Total cut length, complexity score, and bounding box from your DXF.
2. Process time Feed rate by material & thickness → machining minutes × machine hourly rate (₹/hr).
3. Material + quantity Sheet volume cost + quantity multiplier; editable before PDF export.
